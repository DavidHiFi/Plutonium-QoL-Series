**A master repository for my quality of life overhaul mods, covering Zombies on [Plutonium](https://plutonium.pw) across Call of Duty: World at War, Black Ops, and Black Ops II.**

Each mod lives in its own repository. T6-QoL is the current focus; T4 and T5 begin once it is finished.

## Mod manager

[**Quality of Life Series**](https://github.com/DavidHiFi/QualityOfLifeSeries) installs, updates and removes these mods for you. It also manages every other mod in your Plutonium folders across all three games - see what is installed, browse community mods, remove what you no longer want, and launch any game online or in LAN.

[Download it](https://github.com/DavidHiFi/QualityOfLifeSeries/releases/latest) - the setup installs it like any other program, or the portable zip runs as-is. Windows 10 or 11, and nothing else to install.
